Elk is an AI-powered agent platform that helps you automate customer support, capture leads, and engage visitors 24/7. Train your agent on your website content, documents, and knowledge base to provide instant, accurate responses to customer questions.

## What Elk does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Instant answers" icon="message-bot">
    Answers customer questions instantly using your website, documentation, and knowledge base
  </Card>

  <Card title="Lead capture" icon="address-book">
    Captures and qualifies leads with customizable forms and automated follow-ups
  </Card>

  <Card title="Human handoff" icon="user-headset">
    Escalates to human agents when needed with seamless handoff
  </Card>

  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug">
    Integrates with your tools including Slack, Messenger, Zendesk, and more
  </Card>

  <Card title="Auto-sync" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Syncs content automatically to keep your agent up to date
  </Card>

  <Card title="Multi-channel" icon="globe">
    Deploy across website, messaging platforms, and third-party tools
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Key features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="AI-powered conversations" icon="brain">
    Use advanced AI models to understand and respond to customer queries with context-aware answers drawn from your content.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Multi-channel support" icon="messages">
    Deploy your agent on your website, integrate with messaging platforms like Slack and Messenger, or embed in third-party tools.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lead generation" icon="user-plus">
    Collect visitor information with customizable forms, qualify leads automatically, and route them to your sales team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Human handoff" icon="handshake">
    Switch conversations from AI to human agents when needed, with full conversation history and context.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Content management" icon="folder-open">
    Train your agent on websites, PDFs, text files, and more. Set up automatic syncing to keep content fresh.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customization" icon="palette">
    Personalize your agent's appearance, personality, and behavior to match your brand and use case.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
